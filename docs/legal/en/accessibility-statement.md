# Accessibility Statement

**Last updated:** 3 October 2026

## 1. Our commitment

vplus studio is committed to providing equal service to all users, including people with disabilities. We work to make the EasyPen app accessible in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility Adjustments), 5773-2013.

## 2. Accessibility level

The App was developed in line with Israeli Standard SI 5568 and the WCAG 2.1 guidelines at level AA, as far as possible.

- **Date of the last accessibility review:** 25 September 2026

## 3. Accessibility features

### Layout and display

- The interface is in Hebrew (right to left) or English (left to right), according to the device language, and is designed for mobile phones.
- You can enlarge the display through the browser settings. In the document editor, a pinch zooms the pages inside the App (up to 4×) while the toolbars stay in place.
- Colour contrast meets level AA in text and buttons.
- Buttons and touch targets are at least 44 pixels in size.
- The App respects the operating system's "reduce motion" setting.

### Screen readers

- Every button shown as an icon only has a text label for screen readers.
- Form fields are linked to their labels.
- Error and status messages are announced automatically.
- Pop-up windows use the standard `dialog` element: focus moves into them when they open, and the Esc key closes them.

### Keyboard operation

All editing actions in the document are also available from the keyboard, as an alternative to dragging by touch or mouse:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Tab | Move between the buttons, signatures and text boxes in the document |
| Arrows | Move the selected signature or text box |
| Shift + Arrows | Move in a large step |
| + or − | Enlarge or shrink the signature, or the font size of a text box |
| Enter | Edit the selected text box |
| Delete | Delete the selected item |
| Esc | Leave text editing, clear the selection or close a window |

## 4. Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts may not yet be fully accessible:

- **Drawing a signature or drawing on the document** requires a finger, pen or mouse movement. **Alternative:** choose "Text" and type your name instead of the signature.
- **The content of PDF documents** is shown as an image of the page and is not read by a screen reader inside the App. The accessibility of the document content itself depends on whoever created the document.
- **Sharing files to the App** from other apps is available only on devices and browsers that support it (mainly Chrome on Android). **Alternative:** upload the file with the "Upload document" button.

## 5. Accessibility contact

Found an accessibility problem? Please let us know and we will work to fix it:

- **Accessibility coordinator:** vplus studio
- **Email:** [vplus.studio.apps@gmail.com](mailto:vplus.studio.apps@gmail.com)

To help us handle your request, please include:

- a description of the problem;
- the screen or action where you came across it;
- your device and browser;
- any assistive technology you used, if relevant.

Requests will be handled within 14 business days.
