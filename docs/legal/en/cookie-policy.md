# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** 3 October 2026

## 1. Does the App use cookies?

**No.** The EasyPen app does not create cookies and does not use them for tracking, advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

The App does not load components from outside services (such as advertising networks, social networks, fonts or libraries from other websites). So third parties do not create cookies through it either.

## 2. Local storage in the browser

To work, the App stores information in the browser using local storage technologies. This information is **not sent to any server** and is used by the App only:

| Technology | What is stored | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| IndexedDB | Saved signatures (up to 3) | Reusing a signature, if you choose to | Until you delete them |
| IndexedDB | The document open for editing | Showing and editing the document | Until you return to the home screen |
| Cache Storage (Service Worker) | App files | Fast start and working without a network connection | Until a version update or clearing the site data |

## 3. Consent

The local storage described above is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for. It contains no identifying information and is not used for tracking or advertising. That is why the App does not ask for cookie consent.

If cookies or non-essential tools are used in the future, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested in advance, as required by law.

## 4. Managing and deleting

- **Saved signatures:** you can delete any signature on the "My signatures" screen.
- **All local information:** you can delete it through your browser settings. For example, in Chrome for Android: Settings → Site settings → All sites → choose the site → Clear & reset.
- **Removing the App** from your device also deletes the information stored in it.
