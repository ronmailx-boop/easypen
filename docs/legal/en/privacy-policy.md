# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 3 October 2026

## 1. General

This Privacy Policy applies to the use of the EasyPen app (the "**App**" or the "**Service**"), operated by vplus studio (the "**Operator**").

It describes what information is processed when you use the App, where it is stored and what your rights are. It was written in accordance with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (including Amendment No. 13), the Protection of Privacy Regulations (Data Security), 5777-2017, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where it applies.

## 2. In short

- Your documents **never leave your device**. Opening the file, adding a signature or text and creating the signed file all happen in the browser.
- There is no sign-up, no user account, no advertising and no tracking or analytics tools.
- Saved signatures are stored on your device only, and you can delete them at any time.

## 3. Local processing only

Everything the App does happens in the browser on your device. Documents, signatures and the text you add to a document are **not uploaded to the Operator's servers or to any third party**, and the Operator cannot access them.

When you share a PDF file to the App from another app (for example WhatsApp or Gmail), the file is passed to the App directly on your device and does not go through any server.

All the software the App needs, including the libraries for processing PDF files, is loaded from the App's own website and not from outside services.

## 4. Information stored on your device

| Type of information | Purpose | Where it is stored | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saved signatures (up to 3, as PNG images) | Reusing a signature, only if you choose to | IndexedDB in the browser | Until you delete them |
| The document open for editing | Showing and editing the document | IndexedDB in the browser | Until you return to the home screen, when it is deleted |
| App files | Working without a network connection | Browser cache (Cache Storage) | Until a version update or clearing the site data |

A signature is saved for reuse only if you choose the "Save for reuse" option.

The signed file is not stored in the App. It is handed to you through your device's share menu or as a download.

## 5. Information that is not collected

The Operator does not collect, receive or store:

- the content of documents, signatures or text;
- identifying details such as name, email address or phone number;
- usage data, analytics, location or advertising identifiers.

The App does not use cookies. More details are in the [Cookie Policy](cookie-policy.md).

## 6. Website hosting and technical data

The App's files are hosted on Cloudflare, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., based in the United States. Like any web server, the hosting provider may log technical data about each request, such as IP address, browser type and time of access, for operation and security purposes.

The Operator does not receive this data and does not use it. Its handling is subject to [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## 7. Sharing the signed file

Tapping "Save & Share" opens your operating system's share menu. The file goes only to the app or recipient you choose, and from that moment its handling is subject to the terms and privacy policy of that app.

## 8. Data security

- The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
- Information stored on your device is protected by the browser's isolation: other websites cannot access it.
- Information in local storage is **not separately encrypted**. Anyone with access to the unlocked device may be able to access it. We recommend locking your device with a code or biometrics, and not saving signatures on a shared or public device.

## 9. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

The Operator does not receive any personal data from users. Processing on your device is carried out at your request, to provide the service you asked for. A signature is saved for reuse only if you choose so, and you can withdraw this at any time by deleting it.

## 10. Your rights

You have the rights granted by the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law and, where the GDPR applies, the rights under Articles 15–21 of the Regulation, including the right to access, correct and erase your information.

Since the information is stored only on your device, you exercise these rights directly on it:

- **Access and editing:** on the "My signatures" screen in the App.
- **Erasure:** delete a signature on the "My signatures" screen, or delete all the information through your browser settings ("clear site data") or by removing the App from your device.

For questions, you can contact the Operator (section 12). You may also file a complaint with the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority, and users in the European Union may contact the supervisory authority in their country.

## 11. Changes to this policy

The Operator may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page, together with the date of the last update. Material changes to how information is handled will be announced in the App.
