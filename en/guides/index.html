Guides: signing documents on your phone

How to sign a document on your phone, fill in a form and send it back – without a printer or a scanner.

  • Sign a document from WhatsApp Got a contract or a form on WhatsApp? Sign it on your phone and send it back to the same chat in about a minute, for free.
  • Sign a rental agreement on your phone Sign a lease on your phone: a signature on every page, your name, ID number and date filled in, and sent to the landlord or tenant. Free, no sign-up.
