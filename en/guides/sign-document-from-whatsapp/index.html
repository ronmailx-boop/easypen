How to sign a document you got on WhatsApp and send it back

Many documents arrive on WhatsApp these days: a contract, a quote, a registration form. Instead of printing, signing and scanning, sign it on your phone and send the signed copy back to the same chat.

Step by step

  1. Save the file: open the document in WhatsApp. On Android it is usually saved to Downloads or Documents automatically. On iPhone, tap the share button and choose "Save to Files".
  2. Open EasyPen and tap "Upload document". Choose the file you saved.
  3. Sign: tap "Signature", draw it with your finger and place it in the right spot.
  4. Send it back: tap "Save & Share", choose WhatsApp and the same chat. The signed document is sent as a PDF.

Android shortcut: share straight from WhatsApp

If you install EasyPen as an app (in Chrome: "Install app" on the EasyPen home screen, or the ⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen"), it appears in your phone's share menu. Then in WhatsApp you can long-press the document, tap "Share", pick EasyPen, and the document opens ready to sign – no searching through your files.

On iPhone this option isn't available at the moment, so save the file and open it from EasyPen as in the steps above.

Got a photo of a document instead of a PDF?

That works too: EasyPen accepts JPG and PNG photos, including several at once, which are combined into one PDF in order. After signing you send a tidy PDF.

Privacy

The document is never uploaded: opening, signing and creating the signed file all happen on your phone.
