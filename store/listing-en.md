## Full description (max 4000)
EasyPen is the fastest way to sign a document on your phone. Open a PDF or a photo, sign with your finger, add your name or the date, and send it back - in seconds.

Your files stay private: everything happens on your device. Documents are never uploaded to a server, and there's no sign-up.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Sign PDFs with your finger - draw a signature once and reuse it
• Add text: names, dates, ID numbers, checkmarks
• Sign photos: turn JPG and PNG photos into a PDF and sign them
• Combine several files and photos into one document, in any order
• Add more files to an open document, or remove a file
• Draw freely on the document with a pen or a highlighter
• Pick the ink color straight from the document
• Save up to 3 signatures for reuse
• Open password-protected PDFs
• Undo and redo every step
• Share the signed file to WhatsApp, Gmail or any app - or open files shared from them
• Works offline
• Hebrew, English and Russian
